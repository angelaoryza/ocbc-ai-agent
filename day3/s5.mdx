**Learning objectives.** Build the first data source with a test first and a run log, add one AI feature at the lowest rung that works, and show both in a React component with loading and error states.

**Where we left off.** You have a brief, a plan and an empty project. The examples below use the Cash Runway Watcher. If you chose another idea, keep the same steps and write your own known answers first.

## 5.1 Data first, with a test first

Data sets the limit of the system (Day 1, Session 3). Start with the data, check it alone, and only then connect the screen.

For the Cash Runway Watcher, work out the known answers by hand before you build: ending balance in Oct 2026 **Rp 3.330.000.000**; total cash out for the 12 months **Rp 9.860.000.000**; average monthly cash out about **Rp 821.700.000**; months of cover (ending balance divided by average cash out) about **4.1**; the two months with negative net flow, Jan 2026 and Apr 2026.

▶ **Do it:** press `Tab` for the **Build** agent, press `ctrl+x` then `n` for a new chat, and send:

> **Read prototype/AGENTS.md and docs/plan.md. Write the checks first, show me they fail, then build the data step.**
>
> Check 1. Months of cover is the ending balance divided by the average monthly cash out, and for the sample file it is about 4.1. Check 2. A missing or empty file gives a clear error, not a number. Check 3. The two negative months are Jan 2026 and Apr 2026.
>
> Front end: nothing yet.
>
> Back end: a route /api/runway that reads the CSV, works out the numbers in code and returns them. Write one line to logs/app.log for every request: method, path, status and milliseconds. Never log a key.
>
> Data: read only data/cash_flow_sample.csv, copied into the prototype. Build until the three checks pass. Change nothing else.

**What to look for:** (1) all three checks pass after they failed; (2) opening `/api/runway` in the test page at `/docs` shows 3.330.000.000 as the ending balance and about 4.1 months; (3) `logs/app.log` has one line per request; (4) rename the file and call again: a clear error, and a line with the error status in the log.

## 5.2 Show it in a component

▶ **Do it:** send to **Build**:

> **Show the runway numbers on the page, one component per piece.**
>
> Front end: a RunwayCard component with the ending balance, the average monthly cash out and the months of cover; a MonthsTable component with the 12 months, the two negative months marked. Show a loading state while waiting and an error state if the back end fails.
>
> Back end: no change.
>
> Data: read only /api/runway. Don't change anything else.

**What to look for:** (1) the card shows Rp 3.330.000.000, about Rp 821.700.000 and about 4.1 months; (2) the table marks Jan 2026 (−Rp 30.000.000) and Apr 2026 (−Rp 60.000.000); (3) stop the back end: an error message, not a blank page.

## 5.3 One AI feature, at the lowest rung that works

For the Cash Runway Watcher the right rung is a grounded call (Day 1, 5.4): code computes, the model explains. If your idea needs the wiki or an agent, use the same pattern with the tool you chose in 4.2.

▶ **Do it:** write the checks first. Send to **Build**:

> **Before building the summary, write three checks and show me each one fail.**
>
> Check 1. Every number in the summary appears in the /api/runway result. Check 2. The summary never says buy, sell or hold. Check 3. If the model fails, the page still shows the numbers and says the summary is missing.
>
> Front end: nothing yet.
>
> Back end: nothing yet.
>
> Data: nothing yet. Don't build.

▶ **Do it:** now build it. Send to **Build**:

> **Add an AI summary of the runway, with a five-part prompt (ROLE, DATA, GROUNDING RULES, SAFETY RULES, OUTPUT SHAPE), the numbers inside tags, and Pydantic AI for the call.**
>
> Front end: a SummaryBox component under the card, with a loading state, and a note that the app gives no investment advice. If the summary fails, show "The summary is missing" and keep the numbers.
>
> Back end: a route /api/summary. Temperature 0.2, max_tokens 300, timeout 60 seconds, max_retries 2. The key is read only here, from .env. Save the prompt as prompts/summary.md.
>
> Data: the /api/runway numbers only. Build until all three checks pass. Change nothing else.

**What to look for:** (1) a two-sentence summary that names months of cover of about 4.1 and mentions the two negative months, with no number that is not in the data; (2) no buy, sell or hold; (3) switch the key off in `.env` and restart: the numbers still show, with "The summary is missing"; put the key back; (4) all three checks pass; (5) the key appears in no file the browser loads.

**Quick check.** Say your answer to your neighbour first, then compare: (1) Why do we write the data checks before the screen? (2) The model writes "about 5 months of cover". Which check catches it? (3) What does the page show if the AI call fails?
